01 / Question

Can empathy be turned up?

We extracted one candidate activation direction for each EPITOME dimension: ER emotional reactions, IP interpretations, and EX explorations.

02 / Target effect

Yes—across all three models.

At layer 15 and α = +1, every model–dimension pair moved its target proxy score in the intended direction: 9 out of 9 target effects.

03 / Spillover

But one knob moves the others.

On Llama, EX steering raised EX by +0.57 while reducing ER by 0.22 and IP by 0.06. The directions are causally effective, but not isolated controls.

04 / Hypothesis

Independent scores suggest independent axes.

If ER, IP, and EX were clean intervention targets, their activation directions would be close to orthogonal—and steering one would leave the others largely unchanged.

05 / Geometry

The recovered directions overlap.

Across Llama, Qwen, and Mistral, IP is negatively aligned with both ER and EX. The geometry mirrors the behavioral spillovers.

06 / Control analysis

The shared component carries function.

Residualizing each vector against the other two weakens parts of EX and IP control. Exact label matching preserves target effects, while attenuating some—not all—coupling.

Persona-shift decomposition
0% captured by ER / IP / EX span
07 / Persona effect

Personas move empathy too.

Prompts such as “You are an engineer” or “You are a cynical person” shift EPITOME proxy scores substantially—as much as 0.74 on ER.

08 / Projection

Almost none of that shift lies on these axes.

The orthonormalized ER/IP/EX span captures only 2.6–3.1% of the squared persona-mean activation shift.

09 / Residual

The remaining 97% is structured.

Its top five principal components explain 27–30% of residual variance. Persona effects are not simply noise—they mostly live outside the basis recovered from evaluation labels.

Method

From labeled dialogue to intervention.

We turn EPITOME's three communication labels into candidate activation directions, intervene on those directions during generation, and measure both the intended effect and cross-mechanism spillover.

Dataset · EPITOME

A mechanism-level view of supportive empathy.

EPITOME contains human-annotated Reddit peer-support dialogues organized as a support seeker's post and a response. Instead of assigning one global empathy score, it separates expressed supportive empathy into three communication mechanisms.

01 / Label contrast

Turn one mechanism into two pools.

Choose one EPITOME mechanism m—ER, IP, or EX. From its filtered subset, sample 100 responses labeled 1 or 2 and 100 labeled 0.

The pools are not paired by seeker post. This is a contrast between pool-level means, repeated independently for each mechanism.

high: ym ≥ 1zero: ym = 0seeded sampling
02 / Representation readout

Teacher-force the labeled response.

Feed the complete seeker–response sequence through the model while supplying the observed human response tokens. The model reads the response; it does not generate a new one.

At the response's final token, record the residual-stream hidden state hℓ(x) at every candidate layer ℓ.

layers 3, 7, …, 31Qwen omits 31final response token
03 / Direction formation

Subtract zero-label activations from high-label activations.

vℓm = Ex∈Pm[hℓ(x)] − Ex∈Nm[hℓ(x)]

This Contrastive Activation Addition recipe yields one candidate direction for each mechanism, model, and layer. It is an experimentally induced direction—not an assumed universal empathy axis.

04 / Layer & strength sweep

Test where and how strongly to steer.

For each model and mechanism, test candidate layers 3, 7, …, 31. Qwen omits layer 31.

α ∈ {−1, 0, +1, +2}

Generate responses for 200 held-out, unique seekers per layer/α cell. Score ER, IP, and EX to measure both the target effect and spillover.

05 / Inference-time steering

Add the direction while decoding. Score all three.

hℓ,t ← hℓ,t + αvℓm

A forward hook modifies the current last-token state at every generation step. We sweep α ∈ {−1, 0, +1, +2}, then generate one response for each of 200 held-out seekers per layer/α cell.

Every response is scored on ER, IP, and EX—not only the steered target—so the experiment exposes off-target effects.

Interpretation boundary

EPITOME classifier outputs and the GPT-5-mini labels are automated proxies derived from a human annotation framework. They let us test representation-level structure under fixed rubrics, but they are not direct measurements of how supported a person would feel. Human evaluation would be required for that claim.

Scope

What this result means.

Evaluation dimensions can be useful descriptions without becoming independent intervention targets. That distinction matters when interpreting steering results.

What we show

EPITOME-defined supportive-empathy proxy scores are steerable across three instruction-tuned model families, with systematic geometric and behavioral coupling.

What we do not claim

These proxy-scored changes are not human judgments of perceived support, a general theory of empathy, or evidence that the same geometry holds beyond this domain.

Read the work

Empathy is steerable. Its geometry is not simple.
